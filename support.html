•Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the app need location permission?

Android requires location permission for any app to read WiFi network details (SSID/BSSID) or scan nearby devices, even though WiFi Intruder Alerts does not use GPS or track your physical location. This is a platform-level requirement, not something we opt into for tracking purposes.

Does my network data ever leave my phone?

No, with one narrow exception: if you explicitly turn on the optional AI Insights feature in Settings, a limited snapshot of the device you're viewing is sent to our AI provider to generate a plain-language summary. This is off by default. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

I uninstalled the app — what happens to my data?

All device lists, trust profiles, and settings are stored locally in the app's own sandboxed storage. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data/cache from Android system settings) permanently deletes this data from your device. We never held a copy on our servers to begin with.

Can I use this to scan a network that isn't mine?

Only if you have explicit authorization to monitor that network. See Section 4 of our Terms and Conditions.

Is WiFi Intruder Alerts free?

Yes. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.
